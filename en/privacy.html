Privacy

In short: this site does not track you.

No cookies, no analytics. draosmusic.ro is a static website: it sets no cookies of its own, collects no data through the site, and uses no traffic-analysis services. The booking form runs locally in your browser and only composes a message that you then send yourself on WhatsApp. Fonts and images are self-hosted.

External players, only on click. Audio sets are hosted on SoundCloud and Mixcloud; their players load only after you press play — from that moment on, the respective platform’s privacy policy applies (and it may set its own cookies). Videos open directly on YouTube, outside this site.

Contact. The phone and WhatsApp buttons open your own apps — communication happens directly between you and DRAOS, not through this site.

Booking correspondence. Messages sent via WhatsApp, phone or email (name, contact details, event details) are used only for quoting and organising the event, are not shared with third parties, and can be deleted on request.

Consumer disputes. ANPC — ADR (Romania) · EU ODR platform.

Operator. draosmusic.ro is operated by Marginean Dragos-Emil PFA (Romanian sole proprietorship, company ID 46410769), Cluj-Napoca — contact: contact@draosmusic.ro.

Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may keep technical access logs (IP addresses) according to its own policy.
