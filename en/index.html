01 — Who he is

About

Behind the decks since 2001, professionally since 2005 — a quarter of a century of sets in clubs and on big stages, at home and abroad. First contact with music: age 10, in his parents’ club.

A regular guest at UNTOLD Festival (2024–2026, including the Tram Stage alongside DFO Trumpets) and Overnight by UNTOLD. Years of international residencies: Snow-Fest (Les Deux Alpes), Snowland and Italian Snow-Fest (Livigno), Romanian Ski Days (Austria), Bukovel Music Festival (Ukraine). Member of the WAW Recordings label.

Among the pioneers of online live streams in Romania with #D24 (2016) — a 24-hour non-stop live mix. The style: House, Afro House, Ethnic and Progressive — always read from the crowd’s energy.

“One of the most technical DJs in Transylvania.”— CDj Events

His UNTOLD appearance was also covered by the local press in Luduș, his hometown.

06 — Practical

FAQ

What events can I book him for?

Mainly festivals and clubs — from UNTOLD’s Tram Stage to official parties like Transylvania Open. Corporate and private events on request (private events are organised through the CDj Events partners).

How do I book a date?

Message on WhatsApp, call +40 754 968 243 or email contact@draosmusic.ro. Bookings are usually made at least 3 months in advance (exceptions happen), and the date is confirmed with a contract and deposit. Contracts are concluded with Marginean Dragos-Emil PFA (Romanian fiscal registration code / CUI: 46410769).

How much does it cost?

Every offer is tailored — it depends on the event type, duration and location. Pricing is shared on request, quickly, once you send the date and details.

What’s included technically?

He brings the full DJ setup; sound and lights are provided by the venue or promoter (technical rider available on request). Private events can run up to 8 hours; festival and club sets follow the stage schedule. On request: an extended show with live trumpets (DFO Trumpets).

What music will I hear?

House, Afro House, Ethnic House and Progressive — adapted to the crowd and the moment, with a complex technical setup.

Where does he travel?

Based in Cluj-Napoca, available across Romania and worldwide — he has played in France, Italy, Austria and Ukraine.
